      Timing and execution rules

      When confirmation is judged

      4H: when a 4H candle closes, 03:00 · 07:00 · 11:00 · 15:00 · 19:00 · 23:00 UTC+7; with the page open, the auto-scan runs 3 minutes later (around xx:03). 1H: the trigger is read at every scan (the last 12 1H candles), and every 15 minutes while the dashboard is open.

      Which alert means entry

      📍 entered the zone = get ready, not an entry.
      🟢 NEW SIGNAL = entry.
      👀 EARLY MOVER = watchlist only.
      ⚠️ EVENT RISK = entries allowed at reduced size.

      Don't chase

      If price runs away and R:R to TP2 drops below the minimum, the status goes back to WAIT. Wait for price to come back near the planned entry.

      Validity

      A signal is valid for 3 × 4H candles (12 hours). After that it is marked Expired and should not be taken.

      Before you click buy

      1. Top bar says Clear to trade.
      2. Pre-trade checklist on the card is complete.
      3. No bad headline in the news box.
      4. Use the size on the card (already reduced for downtrend, drawdown and event risk).

      After entry

      Mark the card as Take trade. At TP1 scale out and move the stop to breakeven, as in the card's exit plan. Never widen the stop.

      Active-signal backtests

      The same playbook is replayed on the coin's 4H history (about 1,000 candles) using only data up to each signal candle. Entry on the candle close (SMC BUY LIMIT must fill within 6 candles); exit at the stop or TP2, or a time stop after 30 candles (60 for SMC). Fees of 0.1% per side are deducted. Results are information only and never block a signal.

      Edge check: live vs backtest

      No closed trades yet.

      Every signal stores the backtest of its playbook on that coin (same regime when it has 8+ trades). This compares what you actually realised per trade (closed trades in the journal, in R) with what those backtests expected. A gap that is large relative to normal trade-to-trade noise (t ≤ −1) means execution or market conditions differ from the test.       How the early score works

      Each coin outside the RS top 20% is scored 0–100 from five daily signals: RS acceleration (RS rank up 30+ percentile points in 5 days, +35), volume without price (3-day volume ≥ 2× the 30-day average while price moved < 8%, +25), base breakout (close above a tight 30-day range on ≥ 1.5× volume, +20), volatility squeeze above EMA50 (+10) and OBV divergence (+10). Score 35+ is listed; 45+ sends an alert (once per coin per week).

      Backtest on ~1,000 days of Binance daily data for the current universe (101 coins), 21-day horizon: coins scoring 45+ reached the RS top 20% 59% of the time vs 31% for all other coins, averaged +5.5% vs BTC (others −0.8%) and reached a best level of +24% vs BTC on average. Flags came at a median +14% off the 30-day low, versus +22% when coins first enter the RS top. The median flagged coin still lagged BTC (−2.6%), so this is a watchlist, not a buy signal. Results are in-sample and limited to coins that are still liquid today.
